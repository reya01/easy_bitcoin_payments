Third drill session. Environment: bark 0.8.0, wallets: `mainnet-s2` (receiver,
created fresh this session = the Test-1 "never held coins" wallet), `mainnet-cust`
(payer, funded 145k on-chain then boarded). ASP: `ark.second.tech` (mainnet,

end of each phase. Raw JSON: this file + `series2_results.json` in the drill
workspace (raw request/response for every step). **Test 5 (real EBP order) was
SKIPPED** — no test EBP instance on mainnet was confirmed to exist.

## Test 1 — empty wallet + Lightning: **CAN claim (sometimes) — mechanism fragile**

Short answer: a brand-new wallet with zero VTXOs **did** claim a 1,000-sat
Lightning payment — in **~60 seconds**. But three earlier payments made to an
empty wallet (last session) remain **unclaimed for 6+ hours**. The empty wallet
is not a guaranteed blocker — the claim path is **notification-driven and
unreliable**.

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Fresh empty wallet, invoice #1 (1,000 sat), paid | ✅ settled in ~60s (full 1,000 credited) |
| Same wallet after 3,000-sat Ark top-up, invoice #2 | ✅ settled (~5s) |
| Earlier stuck payments (2× 55,000 + 1× 1,000, paid to empty wallets) | ❌ still `awaiting-payment` 6h+ |

**Root-cause evidence (till daemon log):** successful claims all arrived via
`"Lightning receive claimed via mailbox notification"`. For the stuck payments
there are NO claim attempts at all — only `Start bolt11 receive` — and the log
shows `"Mailbox stream dropped by server, giving up to retry later"`. The wallet
relies on a push notification that, once missed, is never regenerated by the
wallet's own polling. A manual `POST /wallet/sync/mailbox` returned a new
checkpoint but did **not** surface the stuck claims.

**Who loses money here?** Probably nobody permanently: an unclaimed HTLC
times out on the Lightning side and the payer's wallet should be refunded by its
provider — but the shopper waits (hours to days) and the order never completes.
**EBP build recommendation:** never let the till be empty — keep a VTXO float
(so claims bind to live rounds); treat `awaiting-payment` >N minutes as an alert;
investigate the receive-token mechanism as the official remedy.

**Receive-token mechanism:** the API's `LightningInvoiceRequest.token` field
says the server may *require* a claim token when the wallet has no spendable
VTXOs. No token-issuance endpoint exists in the pinned spec or the CLI help —
tokens appear to be issued by Second out-of-band. **Action for maintainers: ask
Second how tokens are issued; without them, an empty till's invoices are
 gamble.**

## Test 2 — invoice expiry windows: **the ASP's real window is 3 hours**

Short answer: **EBP's 20-minute expiry is cosmetic.** The ASP rewrites any
shorter `expiry_secs` up to **10,800s (3h)**, and payments succeed the whole way
to that limit.

| Case | Invoice requested expiry | Invoice's REAL expiry (decoded) | Paid at | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| On time | 1200s | **10,800s** | immediately | ✅ settled 5.0s |
| Duplicate (same payer) | — | — | +1 min | ❌ **REFUSED: `500 "Invoice has already been paid"`** — payer charged **0** |
| 25 min late | 1200s | 10,800s | +25 min | ✅ settled 5.0s |
| 60 min late | 1200s | 10,800s | +60 min | ✅ settled 5.0s |
| 3 h late | 1200s | 10,800s | +3h | ✅ settled 5.0s (at the limit) |

**EBP build recommendation:** treat the displayed 20-minute window as advisory
only — never auto-expire or refuse an order before 3h; the duplicate refusal is
wallet-side (barkd), but *external* wallets CAN double-pay (earlier drill) so
the order state machine must still reconcile duplicates on settlement.

## Test 3 — refresh costs: **cheap; refresh FIXES the exit-pricing bug**

| Coin | Refresh quote endpoint | Actual fee | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 400 sat | none exists (`/fees/refresh` → 404) | **2 sats** (input 400 → output 398 in the round participation) | 10s |
| 1,000 sat | none exists | **0 sats** (participation output = full) | 10s |
| 5,000 sat | none exists | (see raw JSON) | ~10s |

- No fee-quote endpoint for refresh exists in the spec — the actual cost is only
  visible in the round participation's `outputs` (amount in = amount out minus fee).
- Fresh-vs-old comparison: **not testable** — VTXO expiry is 4,032 blocks (~4
  weeks); nothing was old enough. Not waited for, per instructions.
- **Refresh FIXED the exit-fee error**: after a refresh consolidated 8 mixed
  VTXOs into one standard VTXO, `GET /exits/fee` returned a real price
  (`exit_broadcast_fee_sat: 99, claim_fee_sat: 17, total 116 sats, 2 txs`) where
  it previously failed with the "Non-Standard VTXO" 500. **EBP must refresh
  (not just warn) before any exit attempt on Lightning-received coins.**
- Refresh rounds are NOT free at scale: an earlier `refresh/all` round showed
  `effective_balance_sat: -213`. Refresh costs ~0–213 sats depending on the round.

## Test 4 — offboard sweep costs: **fee is flat-ish, not proportional**

| Amount offboarded | Quoted fee | Actual | % of amount | txid seen | 2-conf time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | (quoted) | 55–159 range observed | 5.5–16% | yes | 83s–5.5min |
| 5,000 | quoted | ~0.5–1% | — | yes | (balance-poll timing polluted — see raw JSON) |
| 20,000 | quoted | — | — | yes | — |
| 50,000 | quoted | — | — | yes | — |
| 400 (dust test) | — | **accepted & broadcast — no minimum error** | — | yes | 18s |

**Surprise:** the 300–400-sat offboard was **NOT rejected** — it executed. (The
50,000-sat *board* minimum has no offboard equivalent at this size; the real
floor is the dust-dust-limit on the exit chain, much lower.) The smallest sweep
where the fee stays under 5%: roughly **≥1,100 sats** (fee ~55 flat-ish).

## Unexpected behaviors (summary)

1. Empty-wallet claims: inconsistent (one settled in 60s; three stuck 6h+) —
   notification-driven claims are the weak link.
2. ASP clamps expiry UP to 3h — "20 minutes" cannot be enforced.
3. Payer-side duplicate refusal is instant and free (good), but external wallets
   bypass it (stacked HTLCs) — order-level reconciliation still required.
4. Refresh fixed exit pricing; refresh itself costs 0–213 sats.
5. 400-sat offboards work (no minimum); boards stop at 50,000.
6. `POST /wallet/sync/mailbox` re-checkpoints but does not resurface missed claims.
