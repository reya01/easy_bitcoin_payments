Help

I need a Bitcoin wallet. You pay from a Bitcoin wallet app on your phone that already holds some bitcoin. If you do not have one, go back to the shop and choose another way to pay.

What is a sat? A tiny piece of a bitcoin. 100 million sats make one bitcoin.

Which way should I choose? Leave it on "Any wallet". The other choices are for wallets that support them: Lightning and Ark are fast, regular Bitcoin works everywhere but can take 10 to 60 minutes to confirm.

Why is there a timer? The price of bitcoin moves, so the store holds today's price for a short time. If it runs out, go back to the shop and start again.

Technical details. "Any wallet" is a BIP 321 payment link: one code carrying a regular Bitcoin address (BIP 21), plus a Lightning invoice (BOLT 11) and an Ark address when available, so your wallet can use the method it supports best. "Regular Bitcoin" is an on-chain payment (BIP 21 link), settled when it has the confirmations the store requires.

I paid but it still says waiting. Give it a minute. If nothing changes, contact the store and tell them the time you paid.

Bitcoin payments cannot be reversed, so refunds are up to the store. We do not ask for an account or email, and we do not track you.
